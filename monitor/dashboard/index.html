Direct Plan Growth. Returns in %, not annualised except CAGR and alpha. Click a column to sort.

Vol: annualised standard deviation of daily returns. Beta: slope against Nifty 50 daily returns. Lag-adjusted beta adds the previous day's Nifty move, which catches stale prices. Alpha: CAGR minus beta × Nifty CAGR minus (1 − beta) × 7.5% debt, shown once a fund has six months of NAVs. Capture: the fund's summed return on Nifty up (or down) days as a share of Nifty's. AUM: month-end net assets of the latest disclosed portfolio, official from the AMC file or disclosed via Groww's copy of it.

Net equity = stocks + stock futures + index futures, before options. Options appear in disclosures at premium value, not delta, so the option book's real exposure is not in that figure. The NAV-implied exposure (60-day beta × 100) is what the daily NAV says the fund actually carried. A wide gap between the two points to option hedges or timing. Effective N = 1 / sum of squared weights in the stock book. L / M / S use AMFI's Jan–Jun 2026 market-cap list: top 100 large, 101–250 mid, rest small. Shorts count in the band of the stock shorted.

How the data is collected
  • Daily NAVs: AMFI's SIF NAV history service (the one behind amfiindia.com/sif/latest-nav/nav-history), one call per strategy since launch, Direct Plan Growth. AMFI receives NAVs from every SIF by 11 pm IST each business day. The strategy list comes from AMFI's SIF_NAVAll.txt. Every series is also pulled from Groww (same AMFI code) and compared date by date: 4,884 of 4,893 shared dates are identical. AMFI is kept as the record because it has more dates (Groww lacks Altiva's first 3 days, RedHex's first 16 and 8 May 2026) and its values are the ones consistent with the market on the 9 dates that differ. Groww fills a date only if AMFI has not published it yet.
  • Benchmarks: Nifty 50 price index (Yahoo Finance ^NSEI, used for beta). Nifty 50 total return proxied by UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund Direct (AMFI 120716). Arbitrage: Kotak Arbitrage Fund Direct (AMFI 119771). Liquid: HDFC Liquid Fund Direct (AMFI 119091). The 50:50 blend is Nifty 50 plus 7.5% debt, rebalanced monthly. Debt at 6.5% is a fixed 6.5% a year, accrued daily: the plain debt hurdle.
  • Holdings: SEBI requires each SIF to disclose every strategy's full portfolio, derivatives included, as at the end of every alternate month (May, July, September, November, January, March), on its own site and AMFI's within 10 calendar days (SEBI SIF FAQ, A26). Some AMCs publish monthly. The next mandatory disclosure is 30 November, due by 10 December.
  • Each run saves Groww's copy of every hybrid fund's latest disclosed portfolio (every line: stocks, futures, options, debt, REITs, cash, with value and % of NAV, plus net assets), so the history builds up automatically. Checked against Arudha's own 30 Sep file: AUM identical, every asset class within 0.04 points, cap bands within 0.01 points. AMC files add ISINs, quantities (hence trades) and debt ratings, and win when both exist.
  • AUM: month-end net assets of each disclosed portfolio (AMC file or Groww's copy). The 11 funds' 30 Sep figures sum to ₹23,826 cr, 99.5% of AMFI's ₹23,957 cr hybrid long-short total. Industry totals are AMFI's monthly SIF data as reported in the press.
  • Market-cap bands: AMFI's average market capitalisation list for January–June 2026, matched by ISIN, or by name for Groww rows, with former names mapped to current ones (Zomato to Eternal, Adani Transmission to Adani Energy Solutions and so on). Stocks listed after June are unclassified until AMFI's December list.
  • Not public anywhere: daily holdings, daily AUM and option strikes in a usable form. The NAV-implied beta is the closest daily read of a fund's real market exposure.
