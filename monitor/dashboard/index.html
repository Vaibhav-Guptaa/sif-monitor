Pick a period with the window buttons, the date boxes, or by dragging across the strip under the chart (drag the shaded box to move it, click outside it to reset). It applies to every metric. Growth rebases each line to 100 at the period start (or at launch if later); drawdown is measured from the peak inside the period. Trailing 6- and 12-month returns are the plain return over the past 182 or 365 days at each date, so a fund appears once it is that old; the 6-month Sharpe annualises that return, subtracts 5% and divides by the 6-month volatility. Rolling volatility is annualised; rolling 60-day beta needs 40 of the last 60 sessions, 20-day beta 15 of the last 20. The 20-day beta reacts fast but is noisy: one big Nifty day moves it a lot. Vol: annualised standard deviation of daily returns. Beta: slope against Nifty 50 daily returns. Lag-adjusted beta adds the previous day's Nifty move, which catches stale prices. Alpha: CAGR minus beta × Nifty CAGR minus (1 − beta) × 7.5% debt, shown once a fund has six months of NAVs. Sharpe: (annualised return − 5% risk-free) ÷ annualised volatility of daily returns over the same window; since launch shown from three months of NAVs; not shown for debt at 6.5% and the liquid fund, whose near-zero volatility makes it meaningless. 12M high and low: the highest and lowest value of the rolling 60-day volatility and of the absolute rolling 60-day beta over the last 12 months, or since the first full 60-day window for younger funds; hover a cell for the date. Capture: the fund's summed return on Nifty up (or down) days as a share of Nifty's. AUM: month-end net assets of the latest disclosed portfolio, official from the AMC file or disclosed via Groww's copy of it.

How each fund's money is invested

Latest portfolio disclosure, % of NAV. Each bar adds up to 100.

Each fund's equity is split stock by stock: a share held against a short future on the same stock is arbitrage; a share with a call written on it is a covered call, and so on, so every rupee lands in exactly one bucket. Option legs are assumed to cover the whole stock position (disclosures give premiums, not quantities). Beyond the 100 (right of each bar): futures need only margin, so they add exposure without using NAV; long futures act like owning the share, short futures on stocks not held are naked shorts, index futures add or remove Nifty exposure. Options sold on stocks the fund does not hold, and Nifty puts bought, are counted there by number of positions. Black line: share of NAV that moves with share prices before options. White diamond: exposure the daily NAV showed over 60 days (beta × 100). The first row is the family total, weighted by AUM.

Equity exposure before options = stocks + stock futures + index futures (shorts negative). Options appear in disclosures at premium value, not delta, so the option book's real exposure is not in that figure. The exposure the NAV shows (60-day beta × 100) is what the daily NAV says the fund actually carried. A wide gap between the two points to option hedges or timing. Effective number of stocks = 1 / sum of squared weights in the stock book: ten equal holdings give 10. L / M / S use AMFI's Jan–Jun 2026 market-cap list: top 100 large, 101–250 mid, rest small. SIF families: the SEBI types and every fund in them

Each family's rules

Rules for every SIF fund, whatever its family: minimum ₹10 lakh per investor (PAN) across one SIF house's funds, except accredited investors. One fund per family per SIF house. Gross exposure, including derivatives, at most 100% of NAV; a stock held against a future or option on the same stock, same expiry, counts once. At most 10% of NAV in one company's shares, and 15% of a company's paid-up capital across the whole fund house. Option premium paid plus received at most 20% of NAV. Naked short futures, naked written calls and naked written puts all count toward the 25% short cap; covered calls count as hedged. No minimum short is required. Notice period for redemption at most 15 working days; interval strategies must be listed on an exchange. One broad-market benchmark per strategy. Sources: SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds (as on 20 Mar 2026), chapter 21; SEBI FAQ on SIFs, B25, B33, B36, B42.

How the data is collected
  • Daily NAVs: AMFI's SIF NAV history service (the one behind amfiindia.com/sif/latest-nav/nav-history), one call per fund since launch, Direct Plan Growth. AMFI receives NAVs from every SIF by 11 pm IST each business day. The strategy list comes from AMFI's SIF_NAVAll.txt. Every series is also pulled from Groww (same AMFI code) and compared date by date: 4,884 of 4,893 shared dates are identical. AMFI is kept as the record because it has more dates (Groww lacks Altiva's first 3 days, RedHex's first 16 and 8 May 2026) and its values are the ones consistent with the market on the 9 dates that differ. Groww fills a date only if AMFI has not published it yet.
  • Benchmarks: Nifty 50 price index (Yahoo Finance ^NSEI, used for beta). Nifty 50 total return proxied by UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund Direct (AMFI 120716). Arbitrage: Kotak Arbitrage Fund Direct (AMFI 119771). Liquid: HDFC Liquid Fund Direct (AMFI 119091). The 50:50 blend is Nifty 50 plus 7.5% debt, rebalanced monthly. Debt at 6.5% is a fixed 6.5% a year, accrued daily: the plain debt hurdle.
  • Holdings: SEBI requires each SIF to disclose every fund's full portfolio, derivatives included, as at the end of every alternate month (May, July, September, November, January, March), on its own site and AMFI's within 10 calendar days (SEBI SIF FAQ, A26). Some AMCs publish monthly. The next mandatory disclosure is 30 November, due by 10 December.
  • Each run saves Groww's copy of every hybrid fund's latest disclosed portfolio (every line: stocks, futures, options, debt, REITs, cash, with value and % of NAV, plus net assets), so the history builds up automatically. Checked against Arudha's own 30 Sep file: AUM identical, every asset class within 0.04 points, cap bands within 0.01 points. AMC files add ISINs, quantities (hence trades) and debt ratings, and win when both exist.
  • AUM: month-end net assets of each disclosed portfolio (AMC file or Groww's copy). The 11 funds' 30 Sep figures sum to ₹23,826 cr, 99.5% of AMFI's ₹23,957 cr hybrid long-short total. Industry totals are AMFI's monthly SIF data as reported in the press.
  • Market-cap bands: AMFI's average market capitalisation list for January–June 2026, matched by ISIN, or by name for Groww rows, with former names mapped to current ones (Zomato to Eternal, Adani Transmission to Adani Energy Solutions and so on). Stocks listed after June are unclassified until AMFI's December list.
  • Not public anywhere: daily holdings, daily AUM and option strikes in a usable form. The NAV-implied beta is the closest daily read of a fund's real market exposure.
